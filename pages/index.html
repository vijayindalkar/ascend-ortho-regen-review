Questions people ask before they book

Is this treatment safe?

PRP (platelet-rich plasma) uses your own blood, and cellular therapy uses your own cells, never a donor's. Treatment isn't a fit right now if you have active cancer, are in chemotherapy or have an active infection. Dr. Mian goes over the risks with you at your first visit.

What does it cost, and does insurance cover any of it?

Treatment is self-pay. Insurance and Medicare don't cover it or the $395 first visit. Plans start at $1,500, and you'll know the price of yours before you commit to anything. Financing is available, and our office can walk you through it.

Is the $395 refundable?

No. It holds Dr. Mian's time for you, and it pays for the shockwave or laser treatment you get that day. If you go ahead with a treatment plan, the full $395 comes off it.

Am I too old for this?

There's no upper age limit. Dr. Mian treats patients into their 90s.

Does it hurt, and how much time will I miss?

The laser is painless, and you can go straight back to your day. After cellular therapy, plan on taking it easy for a day or two. Dr. Mian tells you what to expect from the treatment he recommends.

How many treatments will I need, and when will I notice a difference?

It depends on the joint and the treatment. PRP usually takes one to three sessions, and many patients notice a difference within one to two months. Dr. Mian tells you what's realistic once he's examined you.

Do I need an MRI or X-ray first?

No. Dr. Mian can do an ultrasound in the office. If you need an MRI, he'll tell you, and it's done at a local imaging center and billed separately.

What if I'm not a candidate?

Dr. Mian will tell you at your first visit and explain what he'd suggest instead. You still leave with the shockwave or laser treatment you had that day.
