A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions people ask before they book.

Clear answers before
you take the next step.

Is this treatment safe?+

Every treatment has risks, and suitability depends on your health and diagnosis. PRP uses platelets from your own blood; cellular therapy uses your own cells. Dr. Mian reviews your medical history, explains the risks and discusses which options may be appropriate at your first visit.

What does it cost, and does insurance cover it?+

The first visit is $395. The visit and regenerative treatment are self-pay and are not covered by insurance or Medicare. Prolotherapy plans start at $1,500. You'll know the price of your recommended plan before you commit. Financing is available.

Is the $395 refundable?+

No. The fee reserves Dr. Mian's time and covers your evaluation and included shockwave or ML7 laser treatment. If you proceed with a regenerative treatment plan, the full $395 comes off it.

Am I too old for this?+

Age alone does not determine candidacy. Dr. Mian considers your overall health, the source of your pain and your goals during your evaluation.

Does it hurt, and how much time will I miss?+

Comfort and recovery time depend on the treatment. Dr. Mian tells you what to expect and any activity restrictions for the treatment he recommends.

How many treatments will I need?+

It depends on the joint, the condition and the treatment. Improvement can take weeks to months. Dr. Mian discusses a realistic treatment plan after examining you. Individual results vary.

Do I need an MRI or X-ray first?+

No. Bring any reports you already have. Dr. Mian can use ultrasound in the office when clinically indicated. If additional imaging is needed, he will explain why and arrange a referral. Outside imaging is billed separately.

What if I'm not a candidate?+

Dr. Mian will tell you at your first visit and explain what he suggests instead. Your $395 visit includes the evaluation and one shockwave or ML7 laser treatment.
